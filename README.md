# godotFinalProj
It's for my college class for the Final.

You are Barnaby, the underworld’s least efficient operative. Blind from birth and possessing no lethal instincts whatsoever, Barnaby was somehow assigned the heist of the century: infiltrating the Vault to steal the legendary Golden Artifact for a large sum of money.

There's just one problem: Barnaby has severe flat feet, wears heavy orthotics, and couldn't kill a fly even if he wanted to. With no sight and zero combat ability, your only tool is your ear, and your biggest enemy is your own feet. Oh, besides all the bad guys who want to kill you for trespassing... You will go through puzzles, be stealthy, and have gadgets to help you along your journey.
